A Makara Thorana is the carved
arch above a temple door:
part crocodile, part fish,
part guardian. This edition
takes its name from Thambapanni,
the copper earth of the island.

One is architecture; one is geography. Both are older than the venue, older than the tickets, older than us. For one evening they share a lawn.

Nine dances,
one spine

From the auspicious drum that opens the evening to the full company finale. Kandyan dance is danced to percussion alone — no playback, no orchestra, no safety net.
